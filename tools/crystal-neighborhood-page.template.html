Crystal Neighborhoods

An Ecology of Mineral Ancestry

Pick a mineral. This plate shows what the simulator's engines say may sit beneath it, replace it, become it, or merely keep it company — read left to right as ancestry to descent. Every line opens its own receipt, and no line is drawn without one.

Reading the lines

What the runtime numbers are. Sighting counts, scenarios and steps come from executing every scenario in this build at the canonical seed and reading what actually grew. They describe Vugg runs, not how often minerals occur together in the ground. A pairing never seen here is not thereby impossible — it means these runs did not produce it.

Preference is not dependency. The engines let almost everything nucleate on bare rock: of species observed in these runs were seen doing it at least once. So a “grows on” line records where a crystal likes to sit, never what it needs. Only relationships in the whole catalogue are strict — a species that cannot appear without its source, or that is its source, transformed.
